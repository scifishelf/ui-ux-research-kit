# Blind expert panel and interviews

Criteria EVAL-02–04. No interviews or expert ratings have been conducted as part of the kit.

Use at least three design/UX experts with anonymized configurations. Supply random bundle IDs, identical fixtures, ordered state/video evidence and correctly sized screenshots without provider names. Calibrate anchors on separate tasks, then collect independent 0–4/null ratings, defect evidence and confidence. Preserve raw ratings; record adjudication separately. Do not average away keyboard blockers or data loss.

An exploratory interview study may recruit 8–12 production frontend practitioners across clients and design-system maturity. Agree consent/access/confidentiality before recruitment. Ask about a recent actual UI ticket, design inputs, token contracts, tested screens/states, failures despite skills/loops, installed PWA versus emulation, correction/token costs, human acceptance, permissions/licenses, incidents/upgrades and independent artifacts. Seek counterexamples rather than only favorite tools.

Analyze a case matrix with role, stack, actual versions, observation versus opinion, negative cases and relevant financial affiliations. Check disputed coding independently where useful. Small interview samples are not representative. Publish only consented, appropriately anonymized real statements; never invent participation or results.
