# Product critique for websites and web applications

Use alongside [web-excellence-audit](../skills/web-excellence-audit/SKILL.md). Load profile, audience, journeys, actual content, brand contract and current screenshots/interaction evidence. A bounded review by the same agent is acceptable; final acceptance also needs human review. Ordinary audits do not require subagents or expert panels.

| Dimension | Observable questions |
| --- | --- |
| Hierarchy and information architecture | Is purpose and the main next step clear? Does order support the user job? |
| Typography and composition | Do line lengths, weights, grid, spacing and density work with real content? |
| Visual identity | Do colors, images, icons and details suit the product? Which decisions make it recognizable? |
| Task success and recovery | Where does the executed flow stall? Are results, correction and resume clear? |
| Responsive behavior and accessibility | Do narrow screens, zoom, keyboard, touch and assistive technology preserve success? |
| Interaction feel | Are responses immediate and predictable? Does motion clarify status and orientation? |
| Robustness and speed | What do states, console/network and traces show? Which field data is missing? |

State strengths and evidenced gaps per dimension. Each gap identifies criterion, route/state, actual artifact and proposed intervention. Unseen behavior stays unresolved. Generator/skill/framework names do not establish quality.

Distinguish measurable violations from reasoned design judgment. Respect approved brand decisions. Do not impose bento grids, dark mode, serif type, glass effects or decorative motion without product relevance.

Feed the observations/action plan with specific improvements in tasks, friction, accessibility and visual concept. Write review prose in the requested report language. No invented effect percentages or automatic excellence certificate.
