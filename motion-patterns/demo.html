Micro-interactions as testable examples

14 patterns with browser animations and deliberately simulated product states. Desktop demos do not replace installed-PWA acceptance.

MP01 · Button and press

Press gives immediate feedback; busy state prevents duplicate completion.

Duration: 80–140 ms; simulated example, not a live operation.

MP02 · Busy and determinate progress
